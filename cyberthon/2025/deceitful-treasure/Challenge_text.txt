You found a glass bottle washed up on the shore...
Within it, lies a scroll of wisdom that hints at a treasure to be found.

"A shell upon the shore, so bright,
Marks progress made, yet lacks true light.

An eggshell, fragile, whispers near,
A clue to guide, a path so clear.

The treasure waits, just one hunt away,
Beyond the waves, where shadows play."
